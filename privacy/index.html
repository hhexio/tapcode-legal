Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 25, 2026

Short version: codes, camera frames, photos, document pages, generated QR codes, PDFs, and scan history are processed and stored on your device. Tapcode does not upload that content to us. Subscription status is handled by the app store and RevenueCat.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Tapcode mobile application (“Tapcode”, “the app”, “we”, or “us”). Tapcode has no account system and no developer-operated application backend.

2. Information processed only on your device

  • Camera and scanned codes. Camera frames are analyzed on-device to find QR codes and supported barcodes. Decoded values may be saved in local scan history.
  • Photos selected by you. A photo you choose is processed on-device to find a code. Tapcode does not upload the photo.
  • Documents and PDFs. Document pages are captured through the operating system’s scanner, then stored and converted to PDF locally. They leave the app only when you choose a system share or save action.
  • Generated codes and preferences. QR payloads, generated images, history, limits, theme, haptic, and sound settings are stored locally.
  • Contacts. For a scanned vCard, Tapcode can open the operating system’s create-contact form. Tapcode does not read or upload your address book.

3. Permissions

Tapcode requests camera access for code and document scanning. It uses the system photo picker to scan an image you select and may request permission to save a generated QR image. Document export uses the system file and share interfaces. Haptics and a short local scan sound can be enabled in Settings. Tapcode does not request microphone or location access.

4. Subscriptions and information sent off-device

Apple or Google processes payments. We do not receive your card or bank details. Tapcode uses RevenueCat to validate purchases and unlock Pro features. When RevenueCat is enabled, its SDK may process an anonymous app user identifier, device type and operating system, last-seen time, purchase history, and an Apple receipt or Google purchase token. Tapcode does not send scanned code values, photos, document pages, PDFs, generated QR payloads, contacts, or local history to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat acts as a service provider for subscription functionality and analytics relating to purchases. Its current practices are described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Store transactions are also governed by the privacy terms of Apple or Google.

5. Advertising and tracking

Tapcode contains no advertising SDK and does not use your scans or device data for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

6. Retention and deletion

Local scans, generated codes, images, and documents remain until you delete them in Tapcode, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Items you save or share outside Tapcode are controlled by the destination you selected. Subscription records are retained by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat as needed to provide purchases, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

You can delete local history and documents in the app. Because Tapcode has no account, we normally cannot identify or retrieve local content. For a RevenueCat data request, contact us through the support link below without posting receipts, purchase tokens, or other private information publicly; we will arrange a private way to obtain the anonymous identifier needed for the request.

7. Security and transfers

Local content is protected by the security controls of your device. Data transmitted for subscription processing uses the security provided by the app store and RevenueCat. RevenueCat states that customer data may be stored in the United States, which may involve an international transfer depending on where you live.

8. Children

Tapcode is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect scanned content or contact information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when Tapcode’s features or service providers change. The effective date above will be updated, and material changes will be reflected in the app or store listing where appropriate.

10. Contact

For privacy or support questions, use Tapcode Support. Do not include private scan content, receipts, purchase tokens, or personal identifiers in a public issue.
